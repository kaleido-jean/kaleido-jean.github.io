About Me

Hi, I'm Jinyao — a robotics engineer in the MRSD program at Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute, working on humanoid loco-manipulation and the unglamorous, essential craft of making real robots work in real buildings.

This site is my personal knowledge base — a "discography" of everything I've learned and built. Each album is a major topic or project, and each track is a concept, technique, or lesson within that domain. Think of it as a creative way to organize and share technical knowledge.

Before graduate school I spent three years at Tesla doing battery abuse testing and production validation. That's where I picked up the question that still drives my work: how do you know a system will hold up in its worst case — and how do you know when your own safety argument is wrong?

Beyond engineering, I'm an art lover who finds inspiration in photography, design, and the intersection of technology and creativity.
