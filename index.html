Robotics Engineer  ·  Art Lover  ·  Lifelong Learner

Jinyao Zhou

MRSD candidate · Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University

I build robots that survive human infrastructure. My capstone team HUMAN is teaching a Unitree G1 humanoid to ride elevators and navigate multi-story buildings — perception, contact-rich manipulation, and behavior-tree missions, all validated on real hardware.

Before CMU I spent three years at Tesla doing battery abuse testing and production validation: pushing systems to their worst case, and learning why safety arguments break outside the lab. That lens still drives what I care about — robot learning that holds up in the real world, and honest evaluation of real robot systems.
